**Reading (2026-10-22 run, main `d5786a7`).** Proving is not the cost: the settlement proof takes 2.8 s and signing plus submission about 1.5 s. The protocol-required waits are Monad finality (settlement block to `payment_verified`, about 55 s) and the funding sends' own finality. Most of the remaining time is configuration and process structure: every native call is a fresh process that re-runs paired verification from scratch, so each `erebus-wallet sync` costs about 31 s even when the wallet is current, and each payment call (funding check, settle, observe) spends about 13 s authenticating the deployment, about 16 s on the observation scan and about 21 s on other native work before its own few seconds of useful work. `check_settlement_funding` and `settle_deal` each sync the wallet first, so one deal pays for that verification four or five times. The driver added no sleeps (0 s). Gas: every send was charged the full 3,000,000 limit (0.306 MON at 102 gwei) while the estimates were about 51k (wrap), 53k (approve), 1.89M (deposit) and 2.73M (settle).

**Targets for WS6 L2** (measured on the same setup): funding (`fund_wallet` call) within 90 s (now 227 s); funded to first delivery within 2 min (now 4.0 min); a second deal within 90 s (now 4.2 min); a new buyer's funding need below 1.6 MON (now 2.14 MON), with per-method gas limits at estimate plus a margin.
