The complete workflow currently runs on Starknet Sepolia. Two independent, role-bound agents connect through MCP. The buyer and seller policies read their budgets, reserves, visible offers, and private note balance. They propose and counter until the buyer accepts a seller-authored offer. Acceptance and shielded payment are one proven state transition, with excess input returned as a change note. Wire version three supports repeat deals in the same channel pair. Packaged recovery tests also completed an exact resubmission and an expired-proof rebuild without double spending. After settlement, a grant encrypted to one registered recipient reconstructs only the selected deal and gives no spending authority.
